What We Offer

Guidance that grows with you

From first-time budgeting to building better financial habits, our tools are built around where you are and where you want to go.

Budget Planning

Create a realistic budget based on your income and expenses so you know how much you can safely spend each month.

Spending Analysis

Upload your bank statement and see where your money is going, with simple suggestions to help improve your spending habits.

Debt Reduction

Build a clear plan for paying down debt and see how different payment amounts can affect how long it takes to become debt-free.

Savings Goals

Set savings goals, track your progress, and stay motivated as you work toward things like an emergency fund, a car, or other purchases.

About Us

Built to make money easier to understand

How2Money was created to help students and young adults take control of their finances without needing to be financial experts. Our goal is to make budgeting, spending, debt management, and basic taxes easier to understand and manage.

Instead of forcing users to juggle multiple financial tools, How2Money brings key features together in one simple platform so users can better understand where their money goes and make smarter financial decisions.

Our Mission
