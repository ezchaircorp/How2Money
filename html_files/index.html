About Us

Built on independence, driven by integrity

Meridian Financial Group was founded in 2007 with a single conviction: that financial advice should be unconflicted. We are a fee-only, fiduciary firm—meaning we do not earn commissions and we are required by law to act solely in your interest.

Our advisors come from backgrounds in tax law, behavioral finance, and institutional asset management. What unites us is a belief that good financial planning is as much about knowing yourself as it is about knowing the markets.
